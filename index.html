Wallpaper Tabs

An animated wallpaper new-tab page with WebGPU shader effects, weather, calendar, and a clock.

Wallpaper Tabs replaces your browser's new-tab page with a living, animated wallpaper rendered with WebGPU shaders. It can optionally show your local weather, a clock, and your upcoming Google Calendar events — all rendered locally in your browser.

What it does

  • Animated WebGPU wallpaper backgrounds with configurable effects (bloom, fog, parallax, and more).
  • Optional current weather and location lookup via the Open-Meteo public API.
  • A clock and customizable fonts.
  • Optional read-only Google Calendar widget that lists your next few events.

Privacy first

Wallpaper Tabs has no backend server. Your settings and any authentication tokens stay in your browser's local storage, and your data is never sent to any server operated by the developer. See the Privacy Policy for full details.
