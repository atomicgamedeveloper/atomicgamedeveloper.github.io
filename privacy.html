Privacy Policy

For the ShaderTab browser extension. Last updated June 2, 2026.

ShaderTab ("the extension") is a browser new-tab extension. This policy explains exactly what data the extension handles. The short version: the extension has no backend server, stores everything locally in your browser, and never transmits your data to the developer.

1. Information we do not collect

The developer operates no servers and runs no analytics, tracking, or advertising. The extension does not collect, sell, or share your personal information. There is no developer-side database of any kind.

2. Data stored locally on your device

  • Your settings (chosen wallpaper effects, fonts, weather location, units, and feature toggles) are saved with the browser's local storage API on your device.
  • Google OAuth tokens. If you connect Google Calendar, the resulting refresh/access token is stored only in your browser's local storage. It is used solely to request your calendar from Google and is never sent anywhere else.
  • Your Google OAuth Client ID and Client Secret. You supply your own Google API credentials, which are stored locally and used only to perform the OAuth exchange with Google.

You can erase all of this at any time by removing or uninstalling the extension, or by clearing the extension's storage in your browser.

3. Google Calendar (optional)

If you enable the calendar widget, the extension requests the https://www.googleapis.com/auth/calendar.readonly scope. This is read-only access: the extension can read your upcoming events to display them, and cannot create, edit, or delete anything. Calendar data is fetched directly from Google's servers to your browser and is shown only on your new-tab page. It is not stored persistently or transmitted to the developer.

4. Google API Services User Data Policy, Limited Use

The extension's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Specifically, Google Calendar data is used only to provide and improve the in-browser calendar widget, is processed locally on your device, is not transferred to others except as needed to provide that feature, is not used for advertising, and is not read by humans.

5. Third-party services

  • Google Calendar API, only if you enable the calendar widget. Governed by Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Open-Meteo (weather and geocoding), only if you enable weather. To fetch a forecast, the extension sends the location text you enter (or its coordinates) to Open-Meteo's public API. See the Open-Meteo terms.

6. Revoking access

  • Revoke the extension's Google access at myaccount.google.com → Security → Third-party access.
  • Uninstall the extension to remove all locally stored data, including any tokens.

7. Children

The extension is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from them.

8. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated date at the top of this page will change. Material changes will be reflected here.
